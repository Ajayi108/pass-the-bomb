How to Play
  1. Stand in a circle with a soft ball.
  2. Press Start and pass the ball while the music plays.
  3. Whoever holds the ball when the explosion sounds loses.
  4. If the ball is in the air, the thrower loses.
  5. Choose elimination, a funny challenge, or a point penalty.
